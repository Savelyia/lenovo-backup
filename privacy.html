Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026. This policy describes the personal Lenovo Backup utility and its informational website.

Purpose and data accessed

Lenovo Backup is operated by its owner for personal backup. With the owner's explicit Google authorization, rclone accesses Google Drive file contents and metadata, such as names, folder paths, sizes, modification times and available checksums, to download and compare backup files. The requested OAuth scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly.

Storage and use

Downloaded files and metadata are used to maintain a local copy on the owner's privately controlled Debian server. OAuth client credentials and access/refresh tokens are stored separately in restricted configuration files. Backup logs may contain file paths and diagnostic information. Local access depends on the owner's operating-system and remote-access permissions; this policy does not claim that the server's disk is encrypted.

Retention and deletion

Unchanged files are skipped and changed files replace their previous local copy. A source deletion does not delete the corresponding local backup. Files and logs remain until the owner removes them. The owner can delete local copies and credentials directly on the server and can revoke Google authorization in Google Account connections. Revocation does not itself delete previously downloaded files.

Sharing and limited use

The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or intentionally share backup contents with unrelated third parties. Google data is transferred from Google to the owner's backup system and may be retrieved by the owner using their authorized devices. It is not published to GitHub Pages or to this website. Lenovo Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This website

This static informational website does not run the backup utility, collect Google account credentials, or provide access to backup files. It has no analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub Pages provides hosting and may process ordinary web-request logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Contact and changes

The operator is the owner of this personal utility. For questions, use the support email shown on the utility's Google consent screen. This policy will be updated if the utility's data practices change.
